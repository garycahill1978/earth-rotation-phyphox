Measuring the Earth's Rotation

A smartphone and MEMS gyroscope experiment using phyphox and a simple 180° reversal method.

Gary Cahill
South East Technological University, Ireland
The Earth rotates at only about 0.00418° per second.
Can a smartphone gyroscope detect it? These experiments use an ABBA reversal sequence to separate the Earth-rotation signal from sensor bias and drift.
ANDROID

Earth Rotation Challenge

Guided measurement using the Android gyroscope. Place the phone flat with the top edge pointing north and follow the on-screen instructions.

The main Earth-rotation result is available after approximately 90 seconds.

The ABBA reversal method

The gyroscope is measured in one orientation, rotated through 180°, measured twice in the reversed orientation, and then returned to its original position.

A1 → B1 → B2 → A2

Reversing the instrument changes the sign of the Earth-rotation component while the sensor bias remains approximately unchanged.

Δω = (A1 + A2) / 2 − (B1 + B2) / 2
