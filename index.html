Android Smartphone Experiment

A guided ABBA measurement using the phone's gyroscope. Place the phone flat with the top edge pointing north and follow the on-screen instructions.

The Earth-rotation result is available after about 90 seconds. An optional sensor-quality test can continue afterwards.
